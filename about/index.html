About Onyx AI Labs

Onyx AI Labs is an independent software studio that builds one-time-payment AI tools for creators, freelancers and small businesses. Instead of renting software through monthly subscriptions, you pay once, run the tool on your own computer or server, and own it outright — full files, no renewal bills, and no data sitting on someone else's server.

Who runs the studio

Onyx AI Labs was founded by Muhammad Mohsin, an independent software developer based in Muzaffargarh, Punjab, Pakistan. The studio is deliberately small: the same person researches each product, builds it, tests the exact files buyers receive, writes the setup guides, films the tutorials and answers support email. That keeps the work honest — if a tool has a limit, the person who hit that limit while building it is the person who documents it.

Why "buy once, own forever"

The studio exists because of a pattern most creators and small businesses know well. A useful tool launches as a subscription. The monthly price looks small, but it never stops: it renews while you are busy, while you are on holiday, and in months when you barely open the tool. Prices rise, features move behind higher tiers, and if the company shuts down or changes direction, the tool you depended on can disappear with your data inside it.

Onyx AI Labs sells software the older way. You pay one time and receive the complete product — not a crippled edition designed to push you into a higher plan. Where a product works with an outside service, it uses your own account or free API key, so your data and your keys stay in your hands. Every product includes a step-by-step START-HERE guide, and every product carries a 14-day money-back guarantee: if it is not right for you, one email is enough for a full refund.

What the studio builds

The catalogue focuses on practical tools for people who make things and run small operations:

  • OnyxIQ, the flagship — a YouTube Growth Studio (browser extension and web app) that audits your channel and scores video titles, descriptions and tags before you publish. A free Basic edition is available alongside the paid Prime edition. Our guide to OnyxIQ explains how it works, with a worked example.
  • Automation kits for WhatsApp and AI agents, built on the open-source workflow tool n8n, so a small business can automate routine customer messages on infrastructure it controls.
  • Business and productivity software — tools for tracking AI subscription spending, drafting contracts and proposals, transcription, and everyday office work, all sold on the same one-time-payment terms.
  • Free tools, including the free edition of OnyxIQ and browser-based utilities, so anyone can start without paying anything.

The full library, with prices and guarantees stated up front, is on our Gumroad store and on the home page.

The Onyx AI channel

Alongside the software, the studio runs Onyx AI, a YouTube channel publishing free tutorials on AI tools and automation. The channel follows the same rule as the products: real tools, shown working end to end, with limits stated plainly instead of hidden. Many products begin life as a problem viewers ask about in the comments, and launch videos record the complete buyer journey — download, install, first run — so you can see exactly what you would be getting before you spend anything.

How to reach us

Questions about a product, support after a purchase, or a business inquiry — the studio reads everything sent to its official email. You will find the address, and a short note on what to include in a support message, on the contact page. Written guides to the main tools and topics live in the guides section.

Support works the same way as everything else here: no ticket system and no call centre — an email to the studio, answered by the person who built the product. And if something in a guide or a product page is unclear, that is worth an email too; unclear documentation is treated as a defect and fixed, not defended.

Start where most people start: the free edition of OnyxIQ costs nothing and shows you the studio's approach — check a channel and score a video before you publish it.
