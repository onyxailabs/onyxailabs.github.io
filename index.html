Is anything here a subscription?
No. Every paid product is a one-time payment. Some tools use your own free accounts (like a free Gemini or YouTube API key) — those keys are yours, and we never see them or charge for them.
Does my data go to Onyx AI Labs' servers?
No — there are no Onyx AI Labs servers. Products run locally in your browser, on your computer, or on a server you control. Privacy here is not a policy promise; it is how the software is built.
What do I actually receive when I buy?
The complete product — full source files where applicable — plus a step-by-step START-HERE guide written from steps that were actually tested. Most buyers are running within an hour.
What if a product isn't for me?
Every product carries a 14-day money-back guarantee. Email us within 14 days of purchase and we refund you in full — no questions asked.
