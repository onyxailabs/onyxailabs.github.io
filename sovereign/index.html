What it does

A sales employee that lives inside your WhatsApp — built on your infrastructure, trained on your business, and supervised by your team on every single message.

Drafting

Replies drafted from your listings

Every draft is written from your actual listings, prices, service areas and approved FAQ answers — in your agency's tone, addressed to the lead by name. No generic chatbot scripts, no invented details.

Speed

Every lead answered in under a minute, 24/7

A personalised draft is ready within a minute of the enquiry arriving — nights, weekends and holidays included. Your approver sees it instantly and sends it with one click, so the buyer hears from you while they are still looking.

Control

This is the core promise, built into the system itself: the send step cannot be reached until a named person on your team approves the draft. The AI never negotiates prices, invents discounts or makes commitments. Your people close the deals.

Every lead's history is kept in one place: what they asked, what was sent, what they replied. When a buyer returns after three weeks, the conversation continues with context — not from zero.

Reporting

Questions, answered straight

Do you need our data, passwords or API keys?

No. Everything runs on your own accounts and your own server. Every key and token is entered by you, into your own systems — we never ask you to send credentials to us in chat, email or screenshots, and we hold none of your data on our side.

What happens if the AI is unsure about a reply?

It drafts, and your team decides. If a question falls outside the approved material, the draft says so and waits — the system never guesses at prices, availability or promises. Sensitive conversations are handed to your people, which is where they belong.

Will this get our WhatsApp number banned?

The system is designed to protect your number, not gamble with it: it works through Meta's official WhatsApp Cloud API, uses approved template messages where Meta requires them, contacts only inbound, opted-in and your own listed leads — never bulk cold blasting — and handles opt-outs immediately. The approval queue also keeps sending at a human pace.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere WhatsApp Business is used. If your buyers enquire on WhatsApp, Sovereign can be installed for your agency — the setup is done remotely, in writing, on your own systems.

What if we stop the Care Plan?

The system keeps running — it is installed on your infrastructure and belongs to you. You simply stop receiving the monthly report, health checks and updates. Nothing is held hostage.
